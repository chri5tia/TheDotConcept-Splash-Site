Samantha and I got married on January 1, 2012.  Because this was just a few short months before I was deployed to Afghanistan, we had a small wedding. Our closest family and friends were at the wedding which was planned in only 2 months. We decided to have our wedding reception after I returned, and took the opportunity to restate our vows to each other.  This was our opportunity to invite all of our family and friends to join in the celebration of our marriage and the return of me and my team from Afghanistan.
